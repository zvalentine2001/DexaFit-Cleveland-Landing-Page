Common Questions

Everything you need to know before you book.

DEXA (Dual-Energy X-ray Absorptiometry) is the clinical gold standard for body composition measurement. It uses two low-energy X-ray beams to precisely distinguish fat tissue, lean muscle, and bone. It's the same technology used in medical research and by elite sports organizations — far more accurate than bioelectrical impedance (InBody) or hydrostatic weighing.
The scan itself takes 6–10 minutes. Plan for 30–45 minutes total, which includes your results review with your DexaFit specialist. You'll leave with your full report and a clear picture of your numbers.
Completely. The radiation exposure is extremely low — roughly equivalent to the background radiation you receive during a 1–2 hour flight, and significantly less than a standard chest X-ray. It is FDA-approved and used safely on thousands of people every day.
Scales and BMI can't tell the difference between fat and muscle. Two people can weigh exactly the same and have completely different body compositions. DEXA gives you a precise clinical breakdown of fat, muscle, and bone — and exactly where each is distributed. It's the difference between a rough guess and a measurement you can actually act on.
Keep it simple: wear comfortable, form-fitting clothing (avoid metal zippers or buttons), skip heavy exercise the day before, and stay normally hydrated. No fasting required. If you're doing repeat scans to track progress, try to schedule at a consistent time of day.
